# Full-Time Status Requirements for Illinois Tech Graduate Students

Source: Graduate Academic Affairs FAQs, https://www.iit.edu/gaa/students/faqs ; Student Employment FAQs, https://www.iit.edu/student-employment/faculty-and-staff/faqs-faculty-and-staff
Last verified: October 2026

## Credit Hour Thresholds
- Graduate students are full-time when registered for at least 9 credit hours per semester.
- Undergraduate students are full-time at 12 credit hours per semester.
- Half-time for graduate students is 4.5 credit hours. This matters for federal aid and loan deferment (for U.S. citizens and permanent residents), even though it is below full-time.

## Special Full-Time Status for Thesis and Dissertation Students
Continuing graduate students registered for one or more credit hours of 591, 600, 691, or PSYC 599 are automatically placed in special full-time status for thesis research or Ph.D. candidacy continuation, even if they are below 9 credit hours.

## Why It Matters for International Students
International students on F-1 or J-1 status must stay full-time to remain compliant with SEVIS. If you need to take fewer than 9 credits as a graduate student, or drop a course that would put you below full-time (for example in your final semester, for medical reasons, or because of academic difficulty), you must apply for full-time equivalency with the Office of Global Services (OGS) before registering below full-time. Dropping below full-time without approval risks a SEVIS status violation. If OGS approves, OGS reports you as full-time for immigration purposes; your academic record still shows the hours you are actually enrolled in.

## Full-Time Status and On-Campus Jobs
International students must be enrolled full-time to be hired for on-campus jobs. Students with an approved OGS exception can still be hired through a paper Payroll Authorization. Teaching and research assistants must be enrolled for at least 6 credit hours.

## Why It Matters for Federal Aid (U.S. Citizens and Permanent Residents)
Graduate students registered half-time (4.5 credits) or more remain eligible for federal aid and continued deferment on existing student loans. Students below half-time can file for a forbearance to keep loans from going into repayment.

## Academic Probation
A cumulative GPA below 3.0 places a graduate student on academic probation. Students on probation are normally limited to 9 credits of registration, and a student becomes eligible for academic dismissal after two consecutive semesters on probation. A cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher is good academic standing. Students on probation also cannot do CPT internships or co-ops.

## Bottom Line for International Students
Before any registration change that would put you under 9 credits in a fall or spring term, talk to OGS first. Get approval before, not after, dropping a course that would put you below full-time.
