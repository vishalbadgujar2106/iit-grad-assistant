# Full-Time Status Requirements — Illinois Tech Graduate Students

Source: Office of the Registrar / Graduate Academic Affairs, Illinois Institute of Technology
Last verified: August 2026

## Credit Hour Thresholds
- Graduate students: full-time status requires at least 9 credit hours per semester.
- Undergraduate students: full-time status requires at least 12 credit hours per semester.
- Half-time for graduate students is 4.5 credit hours — relevant for federal loan deferment eligibility even though it's below full-time.

## Special Automatic Full-Time Status
Continuing graduate students registered for at least one hour of certain research/thesis course codes (such as 591, 600, 691, or PSYC 599) are automatically treated as full-time due to thesis research or PhD candidacy continuation — this is a exception built specifically for students deep into thesis/dissertation work who may otherwise be under 9 credits.

## Why It Matters for International Students
International students on F-1/J-1 status must stay full-time to remain compliant with SEVIS rules. If you need to drop below full-time (e.g., final semester, medical reasons, or academic difficulty), you must apply for full-time equivalency through the Office of Global Services before doing so — dropping below full-time without pre-approval risks a SEVIS status violation. If OGS approves the equivalency, your official academic record still shows the credit hours you're enrolled in; only your immigration-compliance status is adjusted, not your registration.

## Why It Matters for Federal Aid (U.S. Citizens/Permanent Residents)
Graduate students registered at half-time (4.5 credits) or above remain eligible for federal aid and continued deferment on existing student loans. Falling below half-time may require filing for forbearance to keep loans from going into repayment.

## Academic Probation Interaction
A cumulative GPA below 3.0 places a graduate student on Academic Probation, which typically caps registration at 9 credits and can lead to dismissal after two consecutive probation semesters — worth knowing since it can interact with your full-time credit planning.

## Bottom Line for International Students
Before making any registration change that would drop you under 9 credits in a fall or spring term, talk to OGS first. This single rule is one of the most common ways international students accidentally jeopardize their visa status — always get OGS sign-off before, not after, dropping a course that would put you under the threshold.
