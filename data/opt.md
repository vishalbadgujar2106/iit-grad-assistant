# Optional Practical Training (OPT) — Illinois Tech

Source: Office of Global Services (OGS), Illinois Institute of Technology
Last verified: August 2026 — OPT rules and fees change periodically; re-check the OGS website before relying on this for a real deadline.

## What is OPT?
OPT lets F-1 students who have completed (or are close to completing) their degree work in their field of study for up to 12 months, with an additional 24-month STEM extension available for eligible majors.

## Before You Apply
All students must finish the OGS OPT tutorial on Canvas and pass the completion quiz before OGS will accept an OPT application. Keep a screenshot of your quiz completion as proof.

## Application Steps
1. Complete the Canvas OPT tutorial and quiz.
2. Pay the OGS OPT administrative fee (around $100 — confirm current amount on the ISSS portal).
3. Submit an "Initial OPT Request (12 Months)" through the ISSS Portal, including your academic advisor's information for electronic confirmation.
4. Once your advisor approves, OGS typically issues your OPT-recommended I-20 within about 10 business days.
5. Use that I-20 to file Form I-765 with USCIS to receive your Employment Authorization Document (EAD).

## Typical Timeline (example: Spring 2026 graduates)
- Earliest date to request the OPT I-20 from OGS: roughly 90 days before your final semester ends.
- Earliest date to file Form I-765 with USCIS: about 90 days before program end.
- Latest date your I-765 must reach USCIS: within 60 days after your program end date.
Always confirm exact dates for your term on the current OGS OPT page — these shift every semester.

## Fees to Know
- Form I-765 (OPT / STEM OPT extension): online filing is generally cheaper than paper filing; fees have increased in recent years, so confirm the current USCIS fee schedule.
- Optional premium processing (Form I-907) cuts USCIS decision time to about 30 business days for an extra fee, but it is not required — standard processing is fine for most students.

## Reporting Requirements While on OPT
Students on OPT must keep reporting employment and any address/status changes to OGS to remain in compliant F-1 status. Missing these reports can jeopardize status, so treat them as mandatory, not optional.

## Processing Delays
USCIS aims to act within about 90 days but delays happen, especially around spring graduation when application volume spikes. Track your case using your USCIS receipt number on the official USCIS case-status site, and avoid contacting USCIS excessively, as this can slow things down further.

## Who to Contact
Office of Global Services (OGS) handles all OPT/CPT and immigration-status questions — not the academic department, and not Career Services (which handles the internship/employer side of CPT only).
