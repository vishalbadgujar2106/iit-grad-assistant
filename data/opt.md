# Optional Practical Training (OPT) at Illinois Tech

Source: Office of Global Services (OGS), https://www.iit.edu/global-services/student-employment/optional-practical-training-opt and https://www.iit.edu/global-services/student-employment/optional-practical-training-opt/opt-reporting-requirements ; unemployment limits from DHS Study in the States, https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/sevis-help-hub/student-records/fm-student-employment/unemployment-counter
Last verified: October 2026. OPT dates and fees change; re-check the OGS OPT page before relying on this for a real deadline.

## What is OPT?
Post-completion OPT lets F-1 students who have completed (or are completing) their degree work in their field of study for up to 12 months. Students with an eligible STEM degree can apply for an additional 24-month STEM OPT extension afterward (see the STEM OPT notes).

## Before You Apply: Canvas Tutorial
All students must complete the OGS OPT Tutorial in Canvas and pass the tutorial quiz before OGS will accept an OPT application. Save your "OPT Tutorial Quiz Completion Screenshot" as proof.

## Application Steps
1. Complete the OGS OPT Tutorial in Canvas and its quiz, and save the completion screenshot.
2. Pay the $100 OPT administrative fee. The payment link is inside the ISSS request.
3. Submit an "Initial OPT Request (12 Months)" in the ISSS Portal (isss.iit.edu), including your academic advisor's details so they can confirm electronically.
4. After your academic advisor approves, OGS issues your OPT I-20 within 10 business days.
5. Follow the instructions issued with your OPT I-20 to file Form I-765 with USCIS for your Employment Authorization Document (EAD card).

## Timeline and Filing Window
- You can submit the OPT I-20 request in the ISSS Portal up to about 90 days before your program end date.
- Your I-765 must reach USCIS no later than 60 days after your program end date.
- Example from the OGS page for Fall 2026 graduates (final semester ending December 19, 2026): earliest ISSS request September 10, 2026; earliest USCIS filing September 20, 2026; requested OPT start date between December 20, 2026 and February 17, 2027.
- OGS ran Fall 2026 Post-Completion OPT Workshops on Zoom (November 18 and December 14, 2026); links and a recording are on the OGS OPT page.
Always confirm the exact dates for your own term on the current OGS OPT page.

## Fees
- OGS OPT administrative fee: $100, paid within the ISSS request.
- USCIS Form I-765 filing fee (OPT and STEM OPT), as listed by OGS: $470 if filed online, $520 if filed on paper. Confirm the current amount on uscis.gov before filing.
- Optional premium processing (Form I-907): $1,780 on top of the I-765 fee. OGS says it shortens USCIS processing to 30 business days and is not required by OGS or Illinois Tech.

## Photos and Payment Tips
OGS recommends including two passport-style photos with an OPT application. OGS does not recommend one payment method over another, but notes that some students have reported rejections when paying by credit card with Form G-1450; checks, cashier's checks, and money orders are alternatives.

## Processing Delays
USCIS expects to act within about 90 days, but it can take longer. Track your case with your USCIS receipt number on the USCIS case status site, and avoid contacting USCIS excessively, since that can slow things down. A case transfer to another service center does not mean there is a problem.

## Employment Rules While on OPT
Post-completion OPT employment must be related to your field of study and at least 20 hours per week. It can be paid or unpaid.

## Unemployment Limits
Under DHS rules, you can accrue at most 90 days of unemployment during post-completion OPT. SEVIS counts every day without reported employment. If you continue to STEM OPT, the total limit becomes 150 days, which includes the days already used during post-completion OPT.

## Reporting Requirements While on OPT
You must keep reporting to OGS to maintain F-1 status. Most reports are due within 10 days of the change, all through the ISSS Portal:
- Contact information updates (U.S. home address and permanent foreign address)
- Employment reporting: new employment, change of employer, or unemployment, using the "OPT/STEM OPT Employment Reporting" request
- Legal name changes, changes in immigration status, and permanent departure from the U.S.
Post-completion OPT employment reports don't require documents. OGS recommends requesting a reprinted I-20 after updating your employment history.
