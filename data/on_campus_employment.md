# On-Campus Jobs for International Students at Illinois Tech

Source: Student Employment Office, https://www.iit.edu/student-employment/students/faqs-students and https://www.iit.edu/student-employment/faculty-and-staff/faqs-faculty-and-staff
Last verified: October 2026

## Can International Students Work on Campus?
Yes. Illinois Tech's student employment rules state that international students must be enrolled full-time to be hired: at least 9 credit hours for graduate students (12 for undergraduates). Students with a full-time exception approved through the Office of Global Services (OGS) can still be hired, but the hiring department has to complete a paper Payroll Authorization instead of the electronic one. Teaching and research assistants (TAs and RAs) must be enrolled for at least 6 credit hours.

## Hour Limits
- During the fall and spring semesters, student employees may work no more than 20 hours per week in total, across any combination of on-campus jobs.
- During summer semesters and university breaks, students may work up to 40 hours per week.
- You can hold more than one on-campus job as long as the total stays within 20 hours per week during the semester.

## Finding a Job
Start with the On-Campus Jobs section of the Student Employment Office website (iit.edu/student-employment). For off-campus jobs, the Student Employment Office points students to Career Services; remember that off-campus work as an F-1 student needs separate authorization such as CPT or OPT.

## Paperwork and Social Security Number
All hiring paperwork (Form I-9, W-4, and so on) goes to the Student Employment Office, not the hiring department. International students can be hired while their Social Security Number application is pending: submit your Social Security card receipt to the Student Employment Office for I-9 processing, then bring the card itself once it arrives so your I-9 can be completed.

## When You Can Start Working
Do not start working until the Student Employment Office emails you that your Payroll Authorization is approved and your paperwork is complete. Starting before then violates federal labor law and results in termination.

## Pay
Student employees are paid bi-weekly on the university schedule. Direct deposit is strongly encouraged; otherwise paychecks are mailed to your official mailing address. You enter your hours in the Illinois Tech Portal through the Timesheet widget.
