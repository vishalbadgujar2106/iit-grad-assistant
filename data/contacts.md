## What OGS Does
The Office of Global Services (OGS) handles immigration matters for F-1 and J-1 international students at Illinois Tech. That includes CPT, OPT, and STEM OPT requests, full-time equivalency approvals for registering below full-time, new student check-in, and OPT and STEM OPT reporting. OGS is the office to ask about how anything, including an on-campus job, a registration change, or an internship, affects your visa status.

## Submitting Requests to OGS
OGS no longer accepts paper requests. All F-1 and J-1 requests, such as CPT, OPT, and STEM OPT, must be submitted through the ISSS Portal at isss.iit.edu.
