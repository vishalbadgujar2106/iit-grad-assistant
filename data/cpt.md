# Curricular Practical Training (CPT) — Illinois Tech

Source: Office of Global Services (OGS), Illinois Institute of Technology
Last verified: August 2026 — CPT deadlines are set per-term; always confirm current-semester dates on the OGS website.

## What is CPT?
CPT authorizes F-1 students to work in internships, co-ops, or practicums that are directly tied to their major and either required by, or taken for credit within, their curriculum.

## Eligibility
- Must have held lawful full-time F-1 status for at least one full academic year in a degree program.
- Must be in good academic standing — generally a 2.25 GPA minimum for undergraduates and 3.0 GPA for graduate students (some departments set a higher bar). Students on academic probation are not eligible.
- The job offer must directly relate to the major listed on your I-20.
- CPT is authorized per academic term (fall, spring, or summer) — you need a new approval every term, and it will specify full-time (20+ hrs/week) or part-time (under 20 hrs/week) status.
- If you're doing CPT during a required fall/spring term, you must also stay enrolled in your normal full course load alongside the internship credits. If it's your final term, you must be enrolled in at least one additional live course.

## Application Steps
1. Get initial approval from your academic department, Career Services (Experiential Learning), or your school's Career Management Center — Chicago-Kent, Institute of Design, and Psychology students coordinate directly with their academic unit and OGS.
2. Submit a CPT request through the ISSS Portal with your completed CPT form attached. You must already be registered for the associated internship course before submitting.
3. OGS's standard processing time is about 10 business days — this is a strict window, so late submissions risk not getting your I-20 in time.
4. Once approved, you'll receive a new CPT-authorized I-20 and may start work on the listed start date — not before.

## Deadlines Are Term-Specific and Strict
Every semester has its own hard deadlines (e.g., a specific date each summer/fall/spring by which OGS must receive your CPT request for a guaranteed start date). These hard deadlines have historically fallen in late July/early August for fall term and early May for summer term — but treat any specific date as needing verification against the current OGS Key Deadlines page rather than assuming it repeats exactly year to year.

## If Your Employment Changes
If your internship ends early or job details change, you must notify both the Experiential Learning Office and OGS within 10 days. Note that OGS cannot backdate authorization once the approved dates have passed — the SEVIS system doesn't allow it, so timely reporting matters.

## Important Distinction
Twelve or more cumulative months of full-time CPT makes you ineligible for OPT afterward. Part-time CPT does not affect OPT eligibility. ISSS recommends using CPT/OPT (rather than unauthorized work) for any internship, paid or unpaid.
