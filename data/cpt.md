# Curricular Practical Training (CPT) at Illinois Tech

Source: Office of Global Services (OGS), https://www.iit.edu/global-services/student-employment/curricular-practical-training-cpt ; CPT and OPT rule from ICE/SEVP, https://www.ice.gov/sevis/practical-training
Last verified: October 2026. CPT rules changed in 2026; always check the OGS CPT page for the current status before planning an internship.

## 2026 Change: CPT Paused for Many Programs
In 2026, SEVP guidance narrowed when OGS advisers (DSOs) can authorize CPT. CPT must now be a core, required part of the curriculum that all students in the program must complete for the degree. An optional internship or practicum does not qualify on its own. Because of this, Illinois Tech has paused CPT application processing for degree programs that do not have a mandatory practical training component. If you are planning an internship, check with your academic department and OGS whether your program currently qualifies for CPT before accepting an offer.

## What is CPT?
CPT is temporary employment authorization that lets an F-1 student gain practical experience through an internship, cooperative education (co-op), or practicum. The work must be directly related to your major and be part of your curriculum.

## Eligibility
- You must have held lawful full-time F-1 status for at least one academic year in a degree-seeking program.
- You must be in good academic standing: a 3.0 GPA minimum for graduate students and 2.25 for undergraduates. Some departments set a higher bar.
- Students on academic probation cannot pursue or continue co-op or internship programs.
- The job offer must relate directly to the major listed on your I-20.

## Application Steps
Before you start: since 2026, Illinois Tech has paused CPT processing for degree programs without a mandatory practical training component, so first confirm with your department and OGS that your program currently qualifies.
1. Get initial approval from your academic department, Career Services (Experiential Learning), or your school's Career Management Center. Chicago-Kent, Institute of Design, and Psychology students work directly with their academic unit and OGS. Stuart School of Business students receive the CPT form from the Career Management Center after their Symplicity application.
2. Register for the associated internship course. You must already be registered before you submit the CPT request.
3. Submit a "Curricular Practical Training (CPT)" request in the ISSS Portal (isss.iit.edu) with your completed CPT form uploaded. Use the form for your school: Main (Mies) Campus, Psychology, Institute of Design, or Chicago-Kent.
4. OGS issues a new CPT I-20. You may begin working on the approved start date on that I-20, not before.

## Processing Time and Deadlines
OGS estimates about 10 business days to process a CPT request. OGS calls this a strict timeline and cannot accommodate expedited requests, so students who miss the deadlines may not receive their I-20 in time. Deadlines are set each term and posted in the Key Deadlines section of the OGS CPT page.

## Authorization Period and Hours
CPT is authorized one term at a time (fall, spring, or summer), and each term needs a new application or renewal. The CPT I-20 lists the employer, the start and end dates, and whether the CPT is full-time (more than 20 hours per week) or part-time (under 20 hours per week).

## Enrollment Requirements During CPT
During a required term (fall or spring), graduate students must enroll in 9 credits of coursework or research in addition to the internship credits (undergraduates: 12 credits). If the internship falls in your final term, you must also take at least one live course.

## If Your Employment Changes
If your employment ends or your work details change, notify both the Experiential Learning Office (explearning@illinoistech.edu) and OGS (global@illinoistech.edu) within 10 days. OGS cannot backdate authorization dates once they have passed, because SEVIS does not allow it.

## CPT and OPT Eligibility
Under federal (ICE/SEVP) rules, 12 months or more of full-time CPT makes you ineligible for OPT. Part-time CPT does not affect OPT eligibility.
