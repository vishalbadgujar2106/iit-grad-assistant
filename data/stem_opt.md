# STEM OPT Extension at Illinois Tech

Source: Office of Global Services (OGS), https://www.iit.edu/global-services/student-employment/opt-stem-extension , https://www.iit.edu/global-services/student-employment/opt-stem-extension/application-procedures , and https://www.iit.edu/global-services/student-employment/optional-practical-training-opt/opt-reporting-requirements
Last verified: October 2026. Fees and procedures change; confirm on the OGS STEM OPT pages before applying.

## What is the STEM OPT Extension?
The STEM OPT extension adds 24 months of work authorization after your initial 12-month post-completion OPT. You are eligible if you are an F-1 student with a STEM-designated degree from Illinois Tech and you are currently in your initial post-completion OPT period. OGS links to ICE's official STEM Designated Degree Program List so you can check whether your degree qualifies.

## Employer Requirements
STEM OPT employment must be related to your field of study, at least 20 hours per week, and paid. Your employer must be enrolled in E-Verify, you must complete a Form I-983 training plan with the employer, and you must be a bona fide employee of that employer.

## When to Apply
Submit your request no earlier than 90 days before your current OPT EAD card expires. There is no separate OGS cutoff after that, but waiting is risky: OGS cannot expedite requests, and if you submit less than 30 days before your EAD expires, OGS cannot guarantee USCIS will receive your forms in time.

## Application Steps
1. Submit a "STEM OPT Extension (24 Months)" request in the ISSS Portal (isss.iit.edu) with a completed Form I-983, a front and back copy of your OPT EAD card (if you haven't submitted it before), and the $200 STEM OPT administrative fee paid within the request.
2. OGS reviews the request and issues a new STEM OPT I-20 in about 10 business days. You'll get an email when it's ready to download in the ISSS Portal.
3. File Form I-765 with USCIS, online or by mail. The USCIS filing fee listed by OGS is $470 online or $520 on paper.
4. Your application must reach USCIS within 60 days of the STEM OPT I-20 issue date and before your current EAD expires. OGS notes this timing is your responsibility.

## Documents for the I-765 (Online Filing)
A copy of the STEM OPT I-20 endorsed within 60 days before filing, your most recent I-94, previously issued EADs (or passport biographic page and F-1 visa stamp), one passport-style photo, an Illinois Tech transcript or diploma showing your STEM degree, and a copy of your I-797 if you changed status inside the U.S. Mail filing has a longer list on the OGS application procedures page, including two photos and Form G-1145.

## Reporting While on STEM OPT
- Report a new employer within 10 days with a new Form I-983, using an "OPT/STEM OPT Employment Reporting" request.
- Report material changes within 10 days (company name, address, EIN, supervisor contact, job description, or reduced hours) with an updated I-983. Ending employment requires the employer-signed final evaluation on page 5 of the I-983.
- Report home address changes within 10 days with a "Contact Information Update" request.
- Submit a "STEM OPT Validation Report" at 6, 12, 18, and 24 months after your STEM OPT start date, even if nothing changed.
- Submit at least two employer-signed self-evaluations: one at the end of the first year (page 5, top half) and a final one at the end of the second year (page 5, bottom half). OGS emails a reminder 30 days before each is due.
- After you receive your new STEM EAD card, submit an "OPT/STEM OPT EAD Card Submission" request.

## Unemployment Limit
Under DHS rules, the unemployment limit during STEM OPT is 150 days total, which includes any unemployment days used during post-completion OPT.
